The offer in plain terms

What is being sold
A fresh issue of up to 27 crore new shares (face value ₹10). No existing shareholder is selling, so every rupee goes to the company.
Where the money goes
Up to ₹27,500 crore will repay or prepay borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm, the telecom subsidiary. The rest is for general corporate use.
Size of the stake
The new shares are about 2.9% of the company after the issue.
Where it will trade
NSE and BSE. Eligible employees get a discount of ₹54 per share.
Why it matters
It is the first public offering of a Reliance group consumer business, and the largest IPO India has seen. It is larger than Hyundai India (₹27,870 crore) and LIC (₹21,008 crore).

Jio since 2016

From a free 4G offer to the owner of the largest standalone 5G network outside China.

  1. 2016

    Jio launches on 5 September with a 4G-only network and free introductory data and voice. Indian data prices start falling fast.

  2. 2017

    JioPhone brings a 4G feature phone to people who had never used the internet.

  3. 2019

    Jio Platforms is formed in November as the holding company for Jio’s telecom and digital businesses.

  4. 2020

    Meta and Google, along with funds such as KKR and TPG, buy stakes. In total about ₹1.52 lakh crore comes in for 32.97% of the company.

  5. 2022

    Standalone 5G launches in October, built on new spectrum rather than layered on 4G.

  6. 2024

    Mobile tariffs are revised in July, lifting revenue per user across the industry.

  7. 2026

    Jio reaches 524 million subscribers, about 268 million of them on 5G. It files for its IPO in June and goes to market in October.
